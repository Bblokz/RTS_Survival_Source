Steel Seal End Screen

The Steel Seal, round 6: the ink is now physically shaded. The ink pad is a worn, enamelled steel tin with its lid open, a paper label inside, and a felt pad soaked unevenly in ink: wet and glossy where it pools, drier at the edges, with the last impression lifted out of it. The stamp on the order is slightly raised ink in a faint pressed dent, so the letter edges catch the lamp. It starts satin-wet and dries duller over a few seconds. The wet ink on the rubber face has its own clear coat.

Stahlstempel
Victory
The stamp lifts out of the ink tin and slams VICTORY onto the order. It tips up toward the camera and spins once around, which shows off the model. After the spin the letters read correctly, and the stamp holds still for a moment before it leaves.
Defeat
The stamp slams and spins the same way with DEFEAT. Then fire takes the order in from the edges. The stamped letters survive in the middle, and their outside edges keep burning: crawling embers, small flames, smoke and sparks, lit by the fire.
The turn
A stamp face is a mirror image, and no rotation can make mirrored letters read correctly. So the face is swapped for a readable one halfway through the spin, while it points away from the camera and can't be seen. In Unreal that is two face meshes, or one material switch, on that frame.
Desk & props
The MP 40 and StG 44 are the in-game meshes with their textures (colour, normal, metal/rough, AO). The 9 mm and 7.92 Kurz rounds are placed so their footprints never overlap, and get scratched, tarnished PBR materials. The worn steel table and the lamp are as before.

Building it in Unreal

  • Stamp: one steel seal mesh with its PBR textures, plus two face meshes per word: a mirrored one for the slam and a readable one shown after the spin. The print on the paper always uses the readable word.
  • Desk and props: the MP 40 (SM_Ger_InfWeapon_MP40) and StG 44 (SM_Weapon_MP44) are already in the project, so the stage places them as static meshes. Note: the in-game StG 44 material MAT_mp44 only uses the albedo map. Its normal, roughness, metalness and AO maps sit next to the mesh but aren't wired in, and this mock-up uses them. The wall map is one displaced plane with its own normal and roughness maps.
  • Paper and burn: one material for the order texture, the ink mask switched on at the slam, and the burn. The burn comes in from the edges, spares the stamped letters through a keep mask, and keeps a crawling ember band on their edges. Flames, smoke and sparks along those edges come from a Niagara system that spawns on the same mask.
  • On screen while paused: PauseAndLockGame is a real pause. My suggestion is a small debrief stage off the map, with its own camera taking over the view and its actors set to tick while paused, with the buttons as a normal widget on top. Niagara and material Time under pause still need checking in the engine to verify.
